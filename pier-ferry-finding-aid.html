Berkeley Pier-Ferry Project: Finding Aid

Every record in the Berkeley Public Records archive that bears on the proposed ferry terminal and replacement pier at the Berkeley Marina, arranged by series, with what each contains and where to read it.

Compiled November 12, 2026 from archive commit 9eca05f

Before the Council: November 17, 2026, Item 26. A public hearing to certify the project EIR, approve the project and act on its use permit. The archive holds the full packet, including the Final EIR: original (PDF, 127.6 MB, publisher's copy), searchable text. Description.

The collection

Subject
The Berkeley Water Transportation Pier-Ferry Project: a new public pier and ferry terminal at the Berkeley Marina, planned by the City of Berkeley with the San Francisco Bay Area Water Emergency Transportation Authority (WETA).
Dates
2018–2026
Extent
88 documents in 71 descriptions, plus 30 records that mention the project in passing
Creators
City of Berkeley (City Council, City Manager, Parks, Recreation & Waterfront); WETA; the Bay Conservation and Development Commission; consultants to each; members of the public
Repository
Berkeley Public Records, a public archive of City records with every file fingerprinted in a manifest. Files too large for the repository are held at the Internet Archive.
Access
Every record listed is public. Each has its original document, a plain-text version for searching, and an archive record giving its source, retrieval date and sha256.

Scope and content

The records run from the City's 2018 contract for a pier condition assessment and ferry feasibility study to the 2026 environmental impact report and the Council hearing on project approval. They include the City–WETA agreements and the contracts and grants that funded design and environmental review; WETA's business plan for Berkeley service; the parking studies; BCDC design review; and the waterfront plans and Marina Fund reports that anticipate ferry service.

A record is described here when it is about the project or carries a decision, figure or statement about it. Records that only name the project in a list are gathered under Mentions in passing. The descriptions say what each document contains. The Decision Project's analysis is on the pages listed under Analysis built on these records.

Searching the archive yourself

The archive adds records daily, and this aid lists what it held at commit 9eca05f. To find what has arrived since, use two searches.

The project collection. The archive files the project's records under the collection pier_ferry, in L0/sources/pier_ferry (originals) and L1/sources/pier_ferry (text).

Everything else. Council reports, agendas, transcripts and other agencies' records that discuss the ferry are filed by document type, so they turn up only by searching the text. GitHub code search covers most of it. For a complete search, clone the archive and search its text layers:

git clone --depth 1 https://github.com/ericdf/berkeley-public-records
cd berkeley-public-records
grep -rliE 'ferr(y|ies)|WETA' L1 L2 T0

Expect false positives, such as the speaker surnamed Ferry. Cite a record by its archive id and check the copy you read against the sha256 in its archive record.

About this finding aid

Compiled by the Berkeley Decision Project from its source registry for the pier-ferry project. Titles, dates, publishers and file details come from the archive manifest; the descriptions and the Council outcomes come from the documents and annotated agendas themselves. A finding aid is the guide a research library publishes to a collection: what it holds, how it is arranged, and how to use it.

Every figure on this site traces to a primary document. Corrections are welcome and are published when received.
