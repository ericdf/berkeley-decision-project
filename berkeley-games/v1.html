    How to Play

    Controls

    • Left Arrow / A — move one lane left
    • Right Arrow / D — move one lane right
    • Space, P, or Escape — pause / unpause
    • M — mute / unmute
    • Touch — tap the left or right half of the play area

    The loop

    1. A fiscal problem appears overhead.
    2. Overhead boards show what each lane does about it.
    3. Be in the lane you want when you pass under the gate — that commits it.
    4. That lane wears. Worn lanes crack, then pothole, then fail.
    5. In Council Mode, four lanes are barricaded. Steering at one bounces you back — you cannot use it, but you will not crash for trying.
    6. Each stretch of road between bridges is one fiscal year.
    7. The bridge is only there if the BUDGET GAP has reached zero.

    One number

    BUDGET GAP is the hole you have to close before the next bridge. Every fiscal year opens with a forecast showing how it was built.

    Recurring choices close the gap now and make next year open smaller. One-time money closes it for this year only, and is capped so it can never carry a year alone. Borrowing closes it now and reopens it later as debt service.

    Campaign length: 6 bridges. In Council Mode, 4 lanes stay closed.
