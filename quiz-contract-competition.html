Berkeley's Contracting Audit: A Reader Quiz

Berkeley is asking voters for a half-cent sales-tax increase and a $300 million bond. The City Auditor found that 94 contracts, with a listed value of $43 million, had no documented evidence of competition. How much smaller would Berkeley's structural deficit be if the City weren't mismanaging millions of dollars in contracts?

Now you know

Berkeley's audit found a contracting system without basic controls.

In FY2024, 94 of 218 contracts, with a listed value of $43 million, had no documented evidence of competition. Berkeley authorized nearly $85 million in recycling contracts over ten years without seeking bids. In October, Council unanimously passed on first reading an ordinance raising Council's approval threshold from $50,000 to $100,000.

Management without oversight is mismanagement.
