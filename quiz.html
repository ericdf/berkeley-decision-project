Who Pays for the Process?

What Did Measure U Fix?
Measure U is not a fresh governance model. It is a larger authorization built on substantially the same architecture that allowed T1’s voter-facing priorities to remain mutable.

Could Berkeley Have Written a Better Bond?
The choice is not “Measure U or no infrastructure.” Berkeley could preserve flexibility while making material changes measurable, visible, and auditable. Because Measure U does not do so and cannot now be rewritten before November, rejection is the appropriate decision for a voter who treats start/stop/continue discipline as essential.
