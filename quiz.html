Measure Z
Public-bank parcel tax

Does the business case hold up?

Berkeley taxpayers would capitalize a bank intended to serve the broader East Bay. Test the regional cost allocation, fallback lending authority, staffing, underwriting, risk, borrower pipeline, and oversight.

Who Audits the Follow-Through?
Berkeley's accountability problem is not primarily that nobody finds problems. It is whether management corrects them and Council enforces follow-through. Measure U and Measure Z both promise independent audits. Berkeley's audit record suggests that may be less reassuring than it sounds.

An audit can only test what the measure gives the auditor a standard to test. Measures U and V both promise independent audits. So what exactly is Berkeley's Auditor allowed to check?

What Is Berkeley's Economic Development Strategy?
Berkeley has economic-development programs, zoning changes, tourism promotion, business assistance, and an Office of Economic Development. Berkeley says promoting hotels and businesses is part of its response to a $30 million structural deficit. What problem was that supposed to solve — and how would anyone know if it worked?
