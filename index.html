Start with the role of government

A framework for deciding what government should do

Before asking how to pay for something, ask whether the City must ensure it happens, should pursue it with partners, or should decline, transfer, repair or stop it. That question comes first, and it is answerable.

The goal is not less government. It is government capable of doing important things well.

About this project. Berkeley Decision Project is built from the primary record: meeting transcripts, annotated agendas, linked votes, audits, budgets and the City's audited financial statements. Every figure is traceable to a document, and where a figure could not be reproduced from the source it is attributed to, it has been dropped and the omission stated on the page. The project is maintained by a Berkeley resident, not a journalist or an officeholder.

Berkeley Decision Project and Council Scorecards are related civic projects. They share research and a commitment to documented evidence, but serve different purposes: this project develops better approaches to public decisions; Council Scorecards evaluates elected officials.
